02 — How we build it

Four steps, each one signed off before the next.

  1. Step 01

    Interview & observe

    Short sessions with the people who do the work.

  2. Step 02

    Map the current state

    Every step, handover and exception, written down.

  3. Step 03

    Design the future state

    A leaner process with the automation opportunities marked.

  4. Step 04

    Roll out & review

    Introduce the change, then check it holds after a few weeks.
