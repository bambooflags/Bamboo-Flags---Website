Rackspace Spot shutdown: two weeks to leave after a breach

Rackspace closes its Spot cloud service on 8 December 2026, three weeks after a breach. What customers must do, and how to prepare to leave any provider fast.

Bamboo Flags28 Nov 20263 min read

On 24 November 2026, Rackspace told customers that it will close Rackspace Spot, its low-cost cloud service, at 17:00 Central Time (23:00 UTC) on 8 December 2026. The decision came one week after attackers got into the platform. Customers have 14 days to move their workloads and data. After that date, Rackspace says, "customer data will no longer be accessible."

Most small businesses do not run software on Spot. But any business that keeps its website, files or software at one provider can get the same kind of notice.

Leaked credentials gave attackers almost 22 hours inside Spot

Rackspace Spot sold cloud servers through an auction. Customers bid for capacity, and The Register reports that Rackspace advertised savings of up to 90 percent against normal prices. Rackspace built the service with the software company Platform9. It ran applications in containers on Kubernetes.

According to a Rackspace notice that BleepingComputer obtained, "an unauthorized party used leaked credentials to access parts of Rackspace Spot". The access lasted from about 00:20 to 22:00 UTC on 17 November. Rackspace learned about it on 18 November, from a customer report. In a reply on the Spot GitHub forum, Platform9 co-founder Sirish Raghuram wrote that "an observability system was breached using a compromised credential". He called it "a critical error on our part".

In some customer accounts, the attacker could act as a legitimate user. The attacker could start new containers and list servers and storage volumes. Rackspace says the attacker "may have read cluster Secrets and files on storage volumes". Kubernetes Secrets are where applications often keep database passwords and API keys.

Rackspace closed the admin console while it cleaned the environment. Service came back on 21 November, after several delays. Three days later, Rackspace announced the closure. It said the decision gives "a clear path forward for customers". It has not said how many customers were affected, and it did not answer questions from BleepingComputer or The Register.

What Spot customers must do before 8 December

Rackspace's notices, as BleepingComputer reports them, list these steps:

  • Treat Spot API tokens, refresh tokens, kubeconfig files and Kubernetes Secrets as exposed, also without a breach notice.
  • Change every password and API key that was stored in a cluster, such as database passwords and keys for other services.
  • Check the logs of databases, code repositories, cloud storage and other services back to 16 November.
  • Copy data and move workloads to another provider before 17:00 Central Time on 8 December.

The second step does not end with the move. A stolen database password still works after Spot closes, until someone changes it.

Rackspace also closed a service after an attack in 2022

In December 2022, ransomware hit Rackspace Hosted Exchange, its hosted email service. TechCrunch reported that about 30,000 customers used it at the time, and that Rackspace decided to discontinue it.

Providers also retire products after a sale or a change of plan. See our article on the Microsoft To Do retirement for one example. To move in 14 days, a business must already know what runs at the provider, how to copy the data out, and which passwords and keys its systems use.

What to do this week

  1. 01List every cloud and hosting service that your business pays for.Write next to each one what it holds: website, files, email, databases or backups.
  2. 02Keep a recent copy of important data outside each provider.Test once that you can restore from it.
  3. 03Write down where your systems keep passwords and API keys.If a provider is breached, this list tells you what to change.
  4. 04Choose where your most important service would go if you had to move it.Note how long the move would take.
  5. 05If you use Rackspace Spot, start the move now.Change the stored passwords and keys first, then copy your data out before 8 December.
