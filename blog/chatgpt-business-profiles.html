ChatGPT business profiles: how to claim your listing

On 10 November 2026 OpenAI opened Business Hub, where a business can claim its ChatGPT profile for free. Who can claim, how to verify, what to check.

Bamboo Flags11 Nov 20263 min read

On 10 November 2026, OpenAI started a beta called Businesses in ChatGPT. A business can now claim its profile in ChatGPT, correct the information that ChatGPT shows about it, and see how often ChatGPT mentions it. TechCrunch compares the profiles to Google Business Profile.

When a customer asks ChatGPT for a supplier or a shop, the answer can name your business with details that you do not control. At launch, the claim is free.

What Businesses in ChatGPT includes

OpenAI's announcement lists three parts:

  • A business profile, managed in Business Hub. Shops can add a product feed with "current pricing and availability".
  • Insights: "aggregated insights on mentions, website clicks, and common topics" in conversations that cite the business.
  • A Business Agent: an AI agent that answers customer questions about your products and policies, in a separate chat with a label.

OpenAI says that it does not share people's conversations or Business Agent chats with businesses.

Who can claim a ChatGPT business profile

You must own the business or be authorized to represent it. You do not need a ChatGPT Enterprise agreement or an ads account, VentureBeat reports. Local businesses, such as shops and restaurants, can for now claim only an existing listing in the US. Business Agents are only for eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses with a website.

OpenAI's statements about other countries do not agree. The announcement says "eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the U.S." and also "businesses located wherever ChatGPT is available". Outside the US, search for your business in Business Hub to find out.

How to claim and verify your listing

OpenAI's setup guide gives these steps:

  1. Sign in to ChatGPT and open Business Hub from the sidebar, or go to chatgpt.com/business-hub.
  2. Search for your business name and select the matching listing.
  3. Choose a verification method: a code by text or call to the business phone, a code to the business email, a DNS TXT record on your website domain, or a connection to your Shopify store.
  4. Wait for OpenAI's review. The decision comes by email.

The person who manages the listing must then pass an identity check with Persona, a verification company. Without it, they cannot publish changes, connect a product feed, see insights or create an agent. A summary of OpenAI's documents by Flavio Copes says that Persona can ask for a government ID, business registration details and proof of authorization. The name, address and registration or tax number must match official records. OpenAI reviews each profile change before it goes live.

If someone else already claimed your business, select "Dispute ownership" and complete the Persona check.

What a claimed profile does not change

OpenAI's product page, as Copes quotes it, says: "Claiming does not guarantee higher placement or sales." ChatGPT continues to make its own recommendations.

The insights cover the last 28 days. For an online business, a mention counts only when ChatGPT references your website, not only your name. Numbers appear only after activity passes a minimum, so an empty field does not mean zero. Website clicks show interest, not sales.

According to Copes, Business Agents explain and link. They cannot take payments, make bookings or look up orders. OpenAI reviews each agent when you create or change it.

The free price can change. Benji Shomair, OpenAI's vice president of monetization, said that profiles and agents are free at launch and did not exclude fees later, according to VentureBeat, which cites Digiday. Businesses can also pay to make a Business Agent the destination of a ChatGPT ad. OpenAI says these Sponsored Agents are "in a limited test with selected advertisers". For ads in Google's AI answers, see our article on Google AI Mode ads.

OpenAI tried to sell through ChatGPT before. It retired an earlier checkout feature in March 2026.

What to do this week

  1. 01Ask ChatGPT what it knows about your business.Write down the wrong details, such as opening hours, address, prices or services.
  2. 02Search for your business at chatgpt.com/business-hub.If it is in the list, claim it with a verification method that you control.
  3. 03Prepare the documents for the Persona check before you start.The name, address and registration number must match official records.
  4. 04Correct the wrong details in the profile.Use the same data as on your website and your Google Business Profile.
  5. 05Look at the insights again in four weeks.Compare the website clicks with the visits in your own analytics.
