Claude in Microsoft 365 Copilot: on by default from 9 December

From 9 December 2026, Microsoft turns on Anthropic's Claude models in Copilot for UK, EU and EFTA tenants that made no choice. What changes and what to check.

Bamboo Flags12 Nov 20263 min read

On 10 November 2026, Microsoft told administrators that Claude models from Anthropic will be on by default in Copilot for tenants in the UK, the EU and EFTA from 9 December 2026. Until now, these models were off by default in those regions. The change applies to tenants that have not made a choice in the Anthropic setting.

If your business uses Microsoft 365 Copilot or Copilot Chat, your staff can then use Claude models with your prompts and work content, without a decision from you. You have until 9 December to decide.

What changes on 9 December

Microsoft announced the change in two Message center posts. MC1546438 is for EU and EFTA tenants, and MC1546435 is for UK tenants. The links go to copies of the posts on admin news sites. Both give the same result for each current setting:

  • No selection: Claude is enabled for all users.
  • Already enabled: your user and group assignments stay the same.
  • "Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps only": Microsoft retires this option, and the setting moves to all users.
  • Disabled, or specific users and groups: Microsoft keeps your selection.

In most other commercial regions, Claude models are already on by default, according to Microsoft's documentation. Users can select Claude in Researcher and in Edit with Copilot in the Microsoft 365 apps. Copilot Studio and Power Platform can also use the models. Copilot Cowork, an agent that does tasks in Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, SharePoint and OneDrive, uses Anthropic models.

Where Claude processes your data

Anthropic works as a Microsoft subprocessor. Microsoft's Product Terms and Data Protection Addendum apply, and Microsoft says that customer data does not train the foundation models.

The location is different for the UK and the EU:

  • EU and EFTA: Charles Lamanna, a Microsoft executive vice president, announced on 5 November that processing for Claude in Copilot moves into the EU Data Boundary. The Message center post gives early December as the date. Tenants that already use Claude move automatically.
  • UK: UK tenants are not in the EU Data Boundary. The Register reports that, per Microsoft's documentation, Anthropic processes this data in the United States. Check the Microsoft subprocessor list to confirm the location for your contract.

If your contracts or your privacy notice promise that client data stays in the UK or the EU, compare that promise with the location before 9 December.

Models with Data Retention are not part of this change

Some advanced Claude models are labeled "Anthropic models with Data Retention". For these models, Anthropic stores most inputs and outputs for up to 30 days, under Anthropic's own terms and not the Microsoft agreement. These models have a separate setting that is off by default in all regions. The 9 December change does not affect them.

How to change the Anthropic setting

You must have the AI Administrator or Global Administrator role. Microsoft gives these steps:

  1. In the Microsoft 365 admin center, go to Copilot, then Settings, then View all.
  2. Select "AI providers operating as Microsoft subprocessors", then Anthropic.
  3. Select "No users" to opt out, or "Specific users and groups" to limit access.
  4. Select Save.

You can change the setting at any time, also after 9 December. Microsoft warns that some features are available only when Claude is enabled, so a full opt-out can remove features that your staff use.

In the EU, EFTA and UK, a business that enabled Claude earlier under Anthropic's separate commercial terms must opt in again under the subprocessor setting, the documentation says. Copilot Studio and Power Platform have one more control in the Power Platform admin center.
